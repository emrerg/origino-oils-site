Who we serve

Industries we serve

We supply single-origin, fully traceable olive oil to producers who put our oil into their own product, and to companies who bottle and distribute it.

Because every batch comes with complete lab analysis and a known regional origin, we're a natural fit for buyers whose own product depends on the quality and provenance of the oil inside it.

As an ingredient in your product

01

Premium canned fish & seafood

Tuna, sardines, anchovies and mackerel packed in olive oil, where the oil is part of the product's quality promise.

02

Antipasti & preserved vegetables

Roasted peppers, sun-dried tomatoes, artichokes, capers and olive spreads preserved in oil.

03

Cheese in oil

Feta, halloumi, goat cheese and labneh stored and marinated in olive oil.

04

Sauces, tapenade, pesto & dips

Where olive oil is both an ingredient and a flavour carrier, and an "extra virgin" claim needs oil you can stand behind.

05

Specialty bakery & fine foods

Focaccia, crackers and Mediterranean specialties made with, or finished in, olive oil.

06

Ready meals & catering production

Industrial kitchens, in-flight and institutional catering using oil for cooking and finishing.

07

Bottlers & private-label packers

Oil supplied in IBC and flexitank for bottling under your own or a retail brand.

08

Foodservice & premium HORECA

Lab-verified quality for restaurant groups, hotels and caterers who want a named single-origin oil.

09

Importers & regional distributors

A single-origin, traceable line to add to your portfolio.

Quality & specifications

We share full laboratory analysis with every shipment — not a summary, the complete report.

Parameters we report

  • 01Free acidity (%)
  • 02Peroxide value
  • 03UV absorbance (K232, K270)
  • 04Polyphenol content
  • 05Fatty acid profile

Every batch is lab-tested before shipment; reports are available on request before you commit to an order.

Packaging & logistics

Bulk oil is offered in standard export formats: IBC totes and flexitank, sized to your shipment volume. We ship from the Marmara region, with established road and sea logistics to European and Mediterranean ports.
